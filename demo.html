Prepare Feedback Share

See a worked example: reporting for Finance

Finance spends two hours reconciling payments. The first useful slice is a CSV export, demonstrated against the old manual process. A trial suggests value, while a real week of reconciliation still needs validating.

When someone asks for scheduled email delivery, record Investigate, name who will check the need, and agree a follow-up date. Respond with that plan. Sharing the plan does not mean the investigation is complete.

1. Prepare the demo

A concrete user problem gives the audience something to judge.

Separate observed value from hoped-for value. “We do not know yet” is a useful answer.

2. Give feedback a response

    
Capture one request per item. Choose Act, Defer, Decline or Investigate when you know enough, or leave the decision pending. Record the rationale, an owner and a date to follow up.

    
Mark “Response shared” after communicating the response. An acknowledged investigation can remain open; sharing a response does not mark the work complete.
