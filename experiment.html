        A completed example: reviews piling up
        
Observation: Small changes wait until the last days of a sprint for review, leaving the team rushing to finish.

        
Change: For one fortnight, volunteer reviewers check the queue after the daily scrum and take the oldest ready change first.

        
Owner: A volunteer from the team rotates the reviewer reminder.

        
Success signal: Compare how long changes wait for review. Ask at the next retro whether people felt less rushed. Do not measure individual reviewer output.

        
Review after one fortnight: Waiting time improved, but the daily reminder interrupted people during deep work.

        
Decision — Change: Keep checking the queue, use an asynchronous reminder, and review again in a fortnight.
